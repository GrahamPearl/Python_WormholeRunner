9. Alien AI

How seven enemies and a boss make their decisions.

Learning intentions

  • understand how an enemy can be programmed to make choices (AI)
  • see how a finite state machine works
  • understand how an alien decides what it can see
  • compare different movement strategies

Success criteria: I can…

  • describe how three different aliens behave
  • explain what 'leading the target' means
  • explain how a cloak hides you from an alien

What is "AI" in a game?

The aliens are not clever like a person. Each follows a small set of rules, and the rules together make it look as if it is thinking. Game programmers call this AI (artificial intelligence). Your job as a programmer is to choose simple rules that feel interesting to play against.

The shared brain

Every alien class inherits the same update_alien method. It does the same three things each frame: find a target, decide what to do, then move.

What can an alien see?

Before an alien thinks, the game asks: which ship can this alien see? A cloaked ship, or one inside a nebula, is invisible unless the alien is very close. The Seeker has special long-range sensors.

This is a nice example of returning None: "nothing". If every ship is hidden, nearest_ship gives back None, and the alien wanders.

Strategy 1: simple pursuit (Chaser)

The Chaser turns toward you, but only by a limited amount each second. That "turn limit" is why you can dodge it by circling.

Strategy 2: predicting the future (Sniper)

If the Sniper aimed at where you are, its shot would miss when you keep moving. So it works out how long the bullet takes to arrive, then aims at where you will be.

Strategy 5: leaving traps (Minelayer)

The Minelayer does not care about you. It just patrols and drops mines on a timer. The danger is the mines, which are rewarded by the game's collision code.

Try it: tune an alien

In the Game Editor, find Alien: Chaser. Raise its Turn rate to 360 and play level 2. Is it harder to dodge? Now try making the Sniper's Bullet speed very slow. What happens to its aim?

Check your understanding

1. What does nearest_ship return when every ship is hidden?
None.
2. Why does the Sniper aim ahead of your ship?
The bullet takes time to arrive, and your ship moves in that time.
3. What is a state machine?
A program that is in one state at a time and changes state when something happens.
4. Why can you dodge a Chaser by flying in a circle?
It can only turn a limited amount each second.
5. Which alien ignores you completely?
The Minelayer. It patrols and drops mines.
